FINAL VERDICT: TOP 3 STRATEGIES (CONSENSUS FROM 25+ RESPONSES)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. ACTIVE RECALL FLASHCARDS (80/20 RULE)
Strongest consensus (17+ mentions). Junior QCMs recycle the same 20% of concepts.

Do this:
• 15-20 cards per known tech, 5 cards per unknown (definition + purpose only)
• "Why NOT" format: correct answer + why 2 distractors fail
• 3 daily sessions (15 mins each), drill failures immediately
• Day 1: create cards (2h) | Days 2-3: drill 3×15min/day

Skip: Advanced features, edge cases, anything not in 80% of junior questions.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

2. KEYWORD-PATTERN MAPPING
Second strongest (15+ mentions). QCMs test term recognition, not implementation.

Do this:
• One-line per tech: Purpose + 3 keywords (Kubernetes = orchestration, pods, scaling)
• Comparison tables for look-alikes (React vs Angular, MySQL vs MongoDB)
• "If stem says X → answer Y" rules
• Day 1: map all 25 techs (2h) | Day 2: refine (1h) | Day 3: skim (30min)

Skip: Installation, tutorials, anything beyond one-page cheat sheets.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

3. TIMED MOCKS + ERROR LOG
Third strongest (14+ mentions). Builds speed, kills anxiety, trains elimination.

Do this:
• 2 mixed mocks (45-60min each), strict timing
• Log every mistake with 1-line fix, add to flashcards
• Final mock on Day 3 under exam conditions
• Day 1: baseline mock (1h) | Day 2: targeted mock (1h) | Day 3: final (45min)

Skip: Obsessing over scores, redoing same questions without fixing errors.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

3-DAY SCHEDULE (5-6 HOURS/DAY)

Day 1: Mapping (2h) + Flashcards creation (2h) + Baseline mock (1h)
Day 2: Flashcard drills (3×15min) + Mapping refine (1h) + Mock (1h) + Error review (1h)
Day 3: Flashcard review (45min) + Mapping skim (30min) + Final mock (45min) + Rest (1h)

THE RULE: Breadth beats depth. Cover all 25 techs at surface level before any deep dive.
